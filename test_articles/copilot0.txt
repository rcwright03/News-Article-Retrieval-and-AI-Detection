Los Angeles, CA — December 7, 2025 In a move that has sent shockwaves through Hollywood and Wall Street alike, Netflix announced today that it has officially acquired Warner Bros., one of the most storied studios in cinematic history. The deal, valued at an estimated $85 billion, marks the largest merger in entertainment industry history and signals a dramatic reshaping of the global media landscape.
A Streaming Giant Meets a Hollywood Legacy
Netflix, once a DVD rental service turned streaming powerhouse, now gains control of Warner Bros.’ vast library of films and television shows, including iconic franchises such as Harry Potter, The Matrix, and DC Comics. The acquisition also includes Warner Bros. Television, HBO, and Cartoon Network, giving Netflix unprecedented reach across both streaming and traditional broadcast platforms.
“This is about bringing together the best of Hollywood storytelling with the most innovative streaming technology,” said Netflix CEO Ted Sarandos in a press release. “Warner Bros. has defined entertainment for generations, and now we’ll ensure those stories continue to thrive in the digital age.”
Industry Impact
The deal is expected to intensify competition with rivals Disney and Amazon, both of which have aggressively expanded their streaming portfolios in recent years. Analysts predict that Netflix’s new ownership of Warner Bros. will allow it to consolidate content under one umbrella, potentially phasing out HBO Max in favor of a unified Netflix platform.
“This merger is seismic,” said media analyst Carla Ruiz. “It’s not just about content—it’s about cultural dominance. Netflix now controls Batman, Bugs Bunny, and Westeros. That’s a trifecta of global appeal.”
What Comes Next
While Netflix has promised to honor Warner Bros.’ legacy, questions remain about how the acquisition will affect creative freedom, theatrical releases, and existing licensing agreements. Filmmakers and fans alike are watching closely to see whether Netflix will prioritize streaming exclusives or continue to support traditional cinema distribution.
For now, the company has announced plans to create a new division, Netflix Warner Studios, which will oversee both legacy properties and new productions. The first major project under the merged banner is rumored to be a crossover event combining characters from the DC Universe with Netflix’s original hits.
