Los Angeles / New York — September 18, 2025 — In a dramatic turn of events, ABC announced the indefinite suspension of “Jimmy Kimmel Live!” following contentious remarks by its host, Jimmy Kimmel, about the assassination of conservative activist Charlie Kirk. The show, a late-night staple since 2003, was pulled from the schedule just hours after the broadcast.
Why the Suspension Happened
Kimmel’s monologue on Monday criticized what he called the “MAGA gang” — alleging they were exploiting Kirk’s death for political gain. The reaction was swift: the chair of the Federal Communications Commission (FCC), Brendan Carr, reportedly warned broadcasters that airing the show could result in regulatory consequences. Major station owners including Nexstar Media Group and Sinclair Broadcast Group announced that they would stop airing the show “indefinitely.”
An ABC-spokesperson later confirmed the show would be “pre-empted indefinitely.” No firm return date was provided.
Fallout: Advertisers, Viewers, and Accusations of Censorship
The sudden removal of “Jimmy Kimmel Live!” has reverberated beyond the network. The show reportedly generates tens of millions of dollars in advertising revenue — earning ABC substantial income from brand sponsors and its own promotional slots.
Media analysts and free-speech advocates argue the suspension represents a dangerous precedent of political influence over entertainment programming. Some warn it signals a chilling effect: a form of censorship where broadcasters are pressured to silence voices critical of powerful political figures.
Revival — For Some Stations
Just five days later, on September 22, ABC announced “Jimmy Kimmel Live!” would return to air “this Tuesday,” ending the network’s suspension after “thoughtful conversations” with Kimmel.
However, not all affiliates complied. Station owners Nexstar and Sinclair initially refused to resume airing the program — even after ABC restored it — citing ongoing concerns over content and audience standards. By late September, both groups indicated they were ending the boycott, and “Jimmy Kimmel Live!” began returning to their stations.
The “Cancellation” Narrative
Despite the show’s brief return, the character of the interruption — indefinite suspension, pressure from regulators, station boycotts, and a near-week off the air — has led many to frame the event as a de facto cancellation. Political voices celebrated the decision, with one high-profile supporter calling it “great news for America.”
Experts argue the episode fits into a broader pattern of media suppression under increasing political and regulatory pressure — raising questions about the resilience of late-night comedy, freedom of expression, and the independence of broadcast networks.

As of now, “Jimmy Kimmel Live!” has resumed on most—but not all—affiliates. Whether this moment marks a temporary bump in the road or a turning point in late-night media remains uncertain.
