Los Angeles, CA — December 7, 2025 In a surprising move that marks the end of an era in late-night television, ABC announced today that it will be cancelling Jimmy Kimmel Live! after more than 20 years on the air. The decision comes amid shifting viewer habits and the growing dominance of streaming platforms over traditional broadcast programming.
A Staple of Late-Night
Premiering in 2003, Jimmy Kimmel Live! quickly became a cornerstone of ABC’s late-night lineup. Known for its irreverent humor, viral comedy sketches, and celebrity interviews, the show helped define the genre for a new generation. Kimmel’s segments such as “Mean Tweets” and his annual Halloween candy pranks became cultural touchstones, often dominating social media conversations.
“Hosting this show has been the honor of my life,” Kimmel said in a statement. “I’m grateful to ABC, our incredible staff, and most of all to the viewers who welcomed us into their homes night after night.”
Why the Cancellation?
Industry insiders point to declining ratings across the late-night landscape as audiences increasingly turn to streaming services and short-form digital content. ABC executives emphasized that the decision was not a reflection of Kimmel’s performance but rather a strategic pivot toward new formats better suited to modern viewing habits.
“Jimmy has been a trailblazer in late-night television,” said Karey Burke, president of ABC Entertainment. “But as the media environment evolves, we must adapt. This is about reimagining how we connect with audiences.”
What’s Next
Kimmel, who has hosted the show since its inception, has not yet announced his future plans. Speculation ranges from a move to streaming platforms to new ventures in comedy and production. ABC confirmed that a replacement program is in development, though details remain under wraps.
For fans, the cancellation represents the end of a familiar nightly ritual. As one viewer posted on social media: “It feels like saying goodbye to a friend you’ve laughed with for 20 years.”
