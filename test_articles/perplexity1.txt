ABC has canceled “Jimmy Kimmel Live!,” bringing an end to one of the longest-running late-night talk shows on American television and signaling a major shift in the rapidly changing late-night landscape.
The network confirmed the decision in a statement released Monday, citing “evolving audience habits” and a “strategic realignment of late-night programming.” The show, which premiered in 2003, helped define ABC’s late-night identity for more than 20 years with a mix of celebrity interviews, political satire, viral comedy segments, and live music performances.
“Hosting this show has been the privilege of a lifetime,” Jimmy Kimmel said in a prepared statement. “I’m grateful to ABC, to our staff and crew, and especially to the viewers who stayed up late with us all these years. It’s been a wonderful, ridiculous, hilarious ride.”
Ratings for “Jimmy Kimmel Live!” have fluctuated in recent years as traditional late-night shows faced increasing competition from streaming platforms, on-demand clips, and social media. While Kimmel remained a prominent cultural voice, particularly during election cycles and awards seasons, ABC executives have faced mounting pressure to cut costs and adapt to changing viewing behavior.
Industry analysts say the cancellation underscores broader challenges confronting broadcast networks, which once dominated late-night with marquee personalities such as Johnny Carson, David Letterman, and Jay Leno. Today, much of the audience consumes late-night content in the form of short segments on YouTube, TikTok, and other platforms rather than watching full episodes in real time.
ABC has not yet announced a permanent replacement for the time slot. Network insiders say a rotating slate of specials, news-focused programs, and lower-cost unscripted content is under consideration as ABC experiments with new formats.
Production on “Jimmy Kimmel Live!” will continue through the end of the current season, with a series of farewell episodes expected to feature returning guests and retrospective segments highlighting the show’s most memorable moments. Hollywood insiders suggest Kimmel is unlikely to disappear from the spotlight, with potential future projects in streaming, podcasting, and live events already being discussed.
For many viewers, the end of “Jimmy Kimmel Live!” marks not just the conclusion of a show, but the fading of a familiar late-night ritual in an era when the definition of “television” is being rewritten in real time.
